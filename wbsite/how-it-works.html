How It Works

A 4-step process that makes helmet usage non-negotiable before every ride.

01

Wear Helmet

Rider puts on the smart helmet. The embedded FSR-402 pressure sensor detects head contact and activates the Arduino Nano.

02

Sensor Triggers BT

Pressure above threshold (ADC > 300) activates the HC-05 Bluetooth module in MASTER mode to seek the bike's receiver.

03

Signal to Bike

Helmet sends authenticated packet WORN,450,43.99,SR_UNLOCK_2024 every second to the bike's HC-05 SLAVE module.

04

Ignition Unlocked

Arduino validates token → closes relay → bike starts. If helmet removed → relay opens → engine halts within 3 seconds.

BT Protocol

HELMET → BIKE
FORMAT:STATUS,RAW,kPa,TOKEN
WORN:WORN,450,43.99,SR_UNLOCK_2024
OFF:OFFHEAD,12,1.17,SR_UNLOCK_2024
BIKE → HELMET
ACK_OK→ Ignition unlocked
ACK_LOCK→ Ignition locked
TIMEOUT→ 3s no signal → lock

User Instructions

  • Wear helmet properly

    Ensure the FSR sensor makes perfect contact with your head.

  • Turn ON system

    Activate the power switches on both helmet and bike units.

  • Ensure BT connection

    Wait for the green LED to show a solid, linked connection state.

  • Start bike

    Only after the secure connection is confirmed, turn the ignition.

  • Warning

    Do not remove helmet while riding — it will instantly stop the engine.
