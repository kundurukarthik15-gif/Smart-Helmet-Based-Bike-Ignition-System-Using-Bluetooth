Future Improvements

The next generation of SmartRide — planned features to make riding even safer.

GPS Tracking

Real-time location sharing and ride history monitoring. Family members can track the rider's route live.

Accident Detection

MPU-6050 accelerometer detects sudden impacts and triggers an automatic SOS protocol instantly.

Mobile App

Dedicated Android/iOS application to monitor battery levels, sensor stats, and ride history in real-time.

Emergency SMS

Automated SMS dispatch to pre-configured emergency contacts with GPS coordinates on impact detection.

Voice Alerts

Audible voice commands and warnings for the rider via integrated Bluetooth speakers inside the helmet.

IoT Dashboard

Cloud-connected dashboard via ESP8266 for remote monitoring, analytics, and fleet management for organizations.
